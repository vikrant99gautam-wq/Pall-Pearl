Our Story

Every beautiful brand begins with a story. Ours begins with family.

Pall & Pearl was inspired by the woman who has always been a source of love, strength, and inspiration in Founder Avni's life—her mother. The brand's name reflects that special bond and serves as a daily reminder of the values she grew up with: grace, confidence, kindness, and authenticity.

What started as a personal inspiration has blossomed into a fashion brand created for women and girls who love expressing themselves through style. From everyday essentials to statement pieces, every collection is thoughtfully curated to help you feel comfortable, confident, and uniquely yourself.

At Pall & Pearl, we believe fashion is more than clothing—it's a reflection of personality, confidence, and the stories we carry with us every day.

Thank you for being a part of our journey.

We're so happy you're here.
